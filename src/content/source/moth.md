# Moth
## Your world. Your definitions. Checked answers.

Moth compiles supported English facts, definitions, and interpretation skills into a reusable reasoning field. Ask whether an entity meets a definition, then inspect checked English or JSON results.

## How Moth works

1. **Define:** Supply the vocabulary, facts, relationships, and definitions for a task. Supported English skills configure interpretation and composition.
2. **Compile:** Check the interpretation and build a retained field. Existing local BGE assets participate in compilation; updates require a new build and activation.
3. **Ask:** Interpret a supported question against the selected field version.
4. **Check:** Native reasoning establishes a classification and a pinned response plan constructs checked English or JSON. Missing support, refusal, and incomplete execution have distinct meanings.
5. **Replay:** Retain the evidence and replay an earlier episode against its original build within the same runtime.

The scrolling field is a conceptual illustration of dependencies. It does not depict a demonstrated continuous energy optimizer.

## Define purchase readiness

An approved supplier is a supplier who passed inspection. A purchase-ready item has funds available and an approved supplier.

Orderone has funds and its supplier, atlas, passed inspection. These facts support purchase readiness. Ordertwo has funds but its supplier, birch, has only passed audit. The field does not establish purchase readiness for ordertwo; it does not prove that birch failed inspection.

This is an explanatory example, not a live console. Changing the supplier definition to require audit changes the criteria and requires rebuilding.
